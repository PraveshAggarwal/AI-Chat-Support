===================================
  TechFlow Pro — Frequently Asked Questions
===================================

--- GENERAL ---

Q: What is TechFlow Pro?
A: TechFlow Pro is a cloud-based project management and team collaboration platform designed for modern development teams. It combines task management, real-time chat, document collaboration, and CI/CD pipeline monitoring in a single unified workspace.

Q: What platforms does TechFlow Pro support?
A: TechFlow Pro is available as a web application (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge), desktop applications (Windows, macOS, Linux), and mobile apps (iOS and Android). All platforms sync in real-time.

Q: Is there a free tier?
A: Yes! Our Starter plan is completely free and includes up to 5 team members, 10 projects, 1GB storage, and basic integrations. No credit card required.

--- PRICING ---

Q: What are the pricing plans?
A: We offer three plans:
  - Starter (Free): Up to 5 users, 10 projects, 1GB storage, basic integrations
  - Professional ($12/user/month): Unlimited users and projects, 50GB storage, advanced integrations, priority support, custom workflows
  - Enterprise ($29/user/month): Everything in Professional plus SSO/SAML, advanced security controls, dedicated account manager, 99.9% SLA, unlimited storage, API access, audit logs

Q: Do you offer annual billing discounts?
A: Yes, you save 20% with annual billing. Professional becomes $9.60/user/month and Enterprise becomes $23.20/user/month.

Q: Can I switch plans at any time?
A: Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. When upgrading, you'll be charged the prorated difference. When downgrading, the remaining balance is credited to your account.

--- ACCOUNT & SETUP ---

Q: How do I create an account?
A: Visit app.techflowpro.com/signup, enter your email, choose a password, and verify your email address. You can also sign up with Google or GitHub SSO.

Q: How do I invite team members?
A: Go to Settings > Team > Invite Members. You can invite by email, generate an invite link, or sync with your identity provider (Enterprise plan).

Q: Can I import data from other tools?
A: Yes, we support one-click imports from Jira, Asana, Trello, Monday.com, Linear, and Notion. Go to Settings > Import Data and select your source.

--- FEATURES ---

Q: Does TechFlow Pro support Kanban boards?
A: Yes! Each project includes customizable Kanban boards with drag-and-drop cards, custom columns, WIP limits, swimlanes, and card templates.

Q: Can I create custom workflows?
A: On Professional and Enterprise plans, you can create custom workflows with automated triggers, custom statuses, approval gates, and conditional routing.

Q: Does it integrate with GitHub/GitLab?
A: Yes, TechFlow Pro integrates natively with GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, and Azure DevOps. You can link commits, branches, and pull requests directly to tasks, and monitor CI/CD pipelines from within TechFlow.

Q: Is there a built-in time tracker?
A: Yes, all plans include a built-in time tracker. You can start/stop timers on tasks, log time manually, and generate time reports. The Professional plan adds timesheet approvals and billable hours tracking.

Q: Does TechFlow Pro have a REST API?
A: Yes, our comprehensive REST API is available on Professional and Enterprise plans. Full API documentation is available at docs.techflowpro.com/api.

--- SECURITY ---

Q: Is my data secure?
A: Absolutely. We take security very seriously:
  - All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)
  - SOC 2 Type II certified
  - GDPR compliant
  - Regular third-party security audits
  - Two-factor authentication (2FA) available on all plans
  - SSO/SAML support on Enterprise plan

Q: Where is my data stored?
A: Data is stored in AWS data centers. You can choose your preferred region: US East, US West, EU (Frankfurt), or Asia Pacific (Singapore). Enterprise customers can request dedicated infrastructure.

Q: Do you support two-factor authentication?
A: Yes, 2FA is available on all plans. We support authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy), SMS codes, and hardware security keys (YubiKey).

--- SUPPORT ---

Q: How do I contact support?
A: You can reach us through:
  - In-app chat: Available 24/7
  - Email: support@techflowpro.com (response within 4 hours on Professional, 1 hour on Enterprise)
  - Phone: Available on Enterprise plan — call 1-800-TECHFLOW
  - Community forum: community.techflowpro.com

Q: What are your support hours?
A: Our support team is available:
  - Starter: Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 5 PM EST (email only)
  - Professional: Monday-Friday, 8 AM - 8 PM EST (email + chat)
  - Enterprise: 24/7/365 (email + chat + phone + dedicated Slack channel)

Q: Do you offer onboarding assistance?
A: Yes! All new Professional and Enterprise customers receive a free onboarding session with our Customer Success team. We'll help you set up your workspace, import data, and train your team. Enterprise customers get a dedicated onboarding program spanning 2-4 weeks.

--- BILLING ---

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice (NET 30).

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Your data will be retained for 30 days after cancellation. You can export all your data before canceling.

Q: Do you offer refunds?
A: We offer a full refund within the first 14 days of any paid plan. After that, you can cancel at any time but refunds are not available for partial billing periods.

--- TROUBLESHOOTING ---

Q: The app is running slowly. What can I do?
A: Try these steps:
  1. Clear your browser cache and cookies
  2. Disable browser extensions that might interfere
  3. Check your internet connection speed
  4. Try a different browser
  5. If the issue persists, contact support with your browser version and a screenshot

Q: I can't log in to my account. What should I do?
A: Try these solutions:
  1. Make sure you're using the correct email address
  2. Reset your password at app.techflowpro.com/reset-password
  3. Check if your account might be using SSO (ask your admin)
  4. Clear your browser cookies
  5. If still unable to log in, email support@techflowpro.com

Q: I'm not receiving notification emails. How do I fix this?
A: Check these:
  1. Verify your email address in Settings > Profile
  2. Check your spam/junk folder
  3. Add notifications@techflowpro.com to your contacts
  4. Review your notification preferences in Settings > Notifications
  5. Contact support if the issue persists
